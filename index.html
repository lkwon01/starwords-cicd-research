🌠 Backstory

A long time ago, in a galaxy filled with silenced voices, the ancient language of Hangul was nearly forgotten across the stars. A powerful Empire known as The Grammar Order suppressed free speech and erased cultural knowledge.

But a small group of rebels discovered ancient scrolls containing the secrets of Hangul. You are one of them — a young Padawan of language, chosen by the Hangul Force.

Your mission: restore language, defeat confusion, and bring clarity to the galaxy… one Hangul word at a time.
